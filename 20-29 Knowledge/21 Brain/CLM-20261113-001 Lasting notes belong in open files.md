## Claim

Notes meant to last should be stored as files that the owner controls, in open, readable formats such as plain text. Reason: apps become obsolete, but files can outlast them.

## Context and conditions

Applies to personal knowledge that must stay usable for years or decades. It does not say that every tool must use plain text.
