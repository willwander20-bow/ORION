## Open research questions

- Do most digital artifacts sit in closed formats or behind logins? (Brief C3; not measured.)
- Does plain text stay readable on future computers as well as it has since the 1960s? Conditions to check: character encoding and storage media. (BRF-20261113-001 C4. BRF-20261113-003 C4 and C15 predict yes, with no evidence. Neither of these two sources discusses encoding. ## Disputes and gaps

- Two opinion sources, no data. Their independence is not shown. One institutional source (NARA) supports the direction of the claim, but not its reasons, and it gives no data. Look for a source with data or reasons on how long file formats stay readable.
