## Definition

A principle for digital work: work meant to last lives in files that the owner controls, in formats that are easy to retrieve and read. The file matters more than the app that made it.

## Explanation

Apps change, close, or lose support. A file in an open format, such as plain text, stays readable with many tools. A second source says that plain text can be converted into other formats, and that many standard formats (HTML, Markdown, JSON, LaTeX) are themselves plain text. So in that source, "plain text" includes these formats. The source gives no formal definition and no limits for conversion. The principle also asks toolmakers to give users ownership of their data. Steph Ango, the CEO of Obsidian, named it in 2023.
