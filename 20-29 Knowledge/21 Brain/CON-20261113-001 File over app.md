## Definition

A principle for digital work: work meant to last lives in files that the owner controls, in formats that are easy to retrieve and read. The file matters more than the app that made it.

## Explanation

Apps change, close, or lose support. A file in an open format, such as plain text, stays readable with many tools. The principle also asks toolmakers to give users ownership of their data. Steph Ango, the CEO of Obsidian, named it in 2023.
