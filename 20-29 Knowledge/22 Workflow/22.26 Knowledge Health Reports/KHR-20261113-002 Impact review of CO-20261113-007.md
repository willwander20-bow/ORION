### H1

- CLM-001, Context and conditions: "LoC states that digital textual files must contain no measures 'such as digital rights management [DRM] technologies or encryption' that restrict access or use."
- BRF-006 E14 quote ends at "...or encryption)". The E14 limits say: "The tool summary says that the end of the sentence is about measures that restrict access or use."
- The CLM evidence line says: "the end of the sentence comes from a tool summary." The paragraph does not say this.
- CO-007 §4 names the risk: "The words 'that restrict access or use' come from a tool summary." Justin approved the text as written. This finding asks for a verification. It does not say that the approval was wrong.

### H2

- CO-007 §4: "An owner can choose encryption for privacy. The Brief gives no information on that case."
- CLM-001 says that the new condition applies to personal notes only as "an extension", but it does not record the privacy case. MOC-001 "Disputes and gaps" has no line on access measures.
- CLM-001 also says: "ORION applies this claim". So a reader can apply the new condition to ORION notes.

### H3

- CON-002, under the heading "## Related claims": "[[CON-20261113-001 File over app|File over app]]: these methods help keep work in plain files." This line links a Concept, not a Claim.
- CON-002 does not link CLM-001. The connection goes through CON-001 and MOC-001 only. This is a report item from the linked-record scope. It is not an impact of CO-007.
