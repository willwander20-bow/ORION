The two sentences disagree. The first is not LoC wording. CO §6 and §7 name this place as outside the Change Order. This is a finding, not an error of the apply.

### H2

Quotation in MOC-001 ("Evidence for and against", LoC row): "It requires no DRM or encryption that restricts access." The CLM-001 E14 line now says "no DRM or encryption that controls access to or prevents use of the digital work". The meaning is the same; the wording is not. Known and outside the Change Order (CO §3 and §6).

### H3

BRF-006 E14 Quote: "Files must contain no measures (such as digital rights management [DRM] technologies or encryption)". The E14 Limits cell says the end "is about measures that restrict access or use". C9 reads "no DRM or encryption that restricts access or use." The Brain E14 line cites the Orchestrator's check of 2026-11-15, and the CO §4 notes this. Until the Brief is revised, the Brain and the Brief disagree on the wording. This is a traceability gap. The meaning is the same.

### H4

MOC-001: "(evidence `weak`, two opinion sources and one institutional source with indirect support; …". CLM-001: "Confidence is `low`." The MOC statement is correct. The MOC checklist item 8 asks for the status of claims that are not `accepted`. The claim is `accepted`, so there is no gap. This is optional.
